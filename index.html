Special Curricular Programs(SCPs)

Special Curricular Programs (SCPs) are specialized tracks designed by the Department of Education (DepEd) for Junior High School (JHS) learners. They allow students to cultivate their unique talents, interests, and multiple intelligences while pursuing academic basics.
